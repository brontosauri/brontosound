# Legal templates

`terms.md` and `privacy.md` are what a herd shows until its admins publish their own Terms and Privacy Policy, and what "Replace with the template" fills a draft with. Their syntax is in [template.ts](template.ts), their few values in [legalValues.ts](legalValues.ts); operators: [docs/operating.md](../../../../docs/operating.md).
